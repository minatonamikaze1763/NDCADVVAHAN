Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about NDC VAHAN TOOL, its workflow, supported formats and subscription plans.

What is NDC VAHAN TOOL?

NDC VAHAN TOOL is a Chrome extension designed to simplify supported document preparation and upload workflows on VAHAN portal pages.

Which document formats are supported?

The extension supports JPG, PNG, WEBP and PDF files. Actual upload requirements depend on the selected document category and the VAHAN portal's applicable requirements.

Can uploads start automatically?

Yes. Enable Auto-Start Upload to initiate the upload workflow after document preparation and compression are complete.

Can it skip documents already uploaded?

Yes. When Skip Already Uploaded Documents is enabled, the tool checks the VAHAN document workflow and skips documents it identifies as already uploaded.

Can the tool complete final submission?

The optional automatic final submission setting can continue the supported submission workflow after document uploads are processed. Users should verify the final submission status on the official VAHAN portal.

Does the tool provide an upload summary?

Yes. The workflow reports the number of documents processed and the number of documents identified as already uploaded and skipped.

Can I download my prepared documents?

Yes. Use Download ZIP to download the prepared documents together in a ZIP archive.

Does the extension require an account?

Yes. Users access the extension through an authorized account. Account approval, access permissions and configured session limits are managed through the authentication system.

Which subscription should I choose?

The Starter Plan costs ₹499 per month and includes one user account with 3 logins. The Pro Plan costs ₹799 per month and includes one user account with 6 logins. Contact us to discuss a Custom Plan.

How do I activate my subscription?

Contact NDC through WhatsApp at 9052691719 or email nextdimensioncustom@gmail.com to enquire about your preferred plan and subscription activation.

Does the tool replace the official VAHAN portal?

No. NDC VAHAN TOOL assists with supported document-processing and upload steps. Users remain responsible for checking document accuracy and completing official portal requirements.

Will compression affect document clarity?

Compression is designed to reduce file sizes while keeping documents usable. Always review prepared files to ensure important text and details remain readable before submission.
