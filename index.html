Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about NDC VAHAN TOOL, its automation settings, supported formats, and subscription plans.

What is NDC VAHAN TOOL?

NDC VAHAN TOOL is a Chrome extension designed to simplify repetitive document preparation and upload workflows on supported VAHAN portal pages.

Which document formats are supported?

The tool supports JPG, PNG, and PDF files. Actual upload requirements depend on the selected document category and the VAHAN portal's applicable requirements.

Can uploads start automatically?

Yes. Enable the Auto-Start Upload setting to initiate the upload workflow after document compression and preparation are complete.

Will compression affect document clarity?

Image compression is designed to balance file-size reduction with readability. Always review prepared documents to ensure important text and details remain clear before submission.

Which subscription should I choose?

The Starter Plan costs ₹499 per month and includes one user account with 3 logins. The Pro Plan costs ₹799 per month and includes one user account with 6 logins. If you need a different login allowance or a tailored subscription arrangement, contact us to discuss a Custom Plan.

How do I activate my subscription?

Contact NDC through WhatsApp at 9052691719 or email nextdimensioncustom@gmail.com to enquire about your preferred plan and subscription activation.

Does the tool replace the official VAHAN portal?

No. NDC VAHAN TOOL assists with supported document-processing and upload steps. Users remain responsible for checking document accuracy and completing official portal requirements.
