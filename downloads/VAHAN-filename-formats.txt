The extension recognizes 500 additional filename aliases across these subcategories. Existing aliases remain supported. Matching ignores uppercase/lowercase and filename separators. Generated aliases were checked for uniqueness and cross-category collisions.

Tip: Keep the correct customer name and document type in every filename. Matching is case-insensitive and ignores spaces, underscores, and hyphens. Examples: Customer_Aadhaar.jpg, Bike_Invoice.pdf, Form22.pdf.
