Add .jpg, .png, .webp or .pdf to match your actual file format. Names are case-insensitive. Review assigned categories before uploading.

When AUTO img to PDF CONVERTION is enabled, images are compressed first and then converted to PDF. Final files must fit within 400 KB (400,000 bytes); Form 20 uses its smaller existing limit. The setting is disabled by default.
