# Customer review showcase

Publish index.html, reviews-showcase.js and reviews-showcase.css after deploying
the reviews-enabled backend. The page loads real published reviews from
GET /api/public/reviews and published logos from /api/public/review-logos/:id.

Manage reviews in the superuser panel’s Reviews section. Drafts and archived
reviews stay hidden. The customer logo, name, company/role, feedback, optional
rating and user-since month/year are displayed using the existing root theme.
There are no invented reviews or automatically assigned star ratings.

See the backend REVIEWS.md for deployment, management and API details.
