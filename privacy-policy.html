01 Overview

VAHAN NDC TOOL is developed as a productivity utility for document preparation and upload workflows. This policy explains the information the extension may process and how that information is used to provide its features.

02 Information We Process

Document processing and NDC account records serve different purposes. Depending on the features you use, the following information may be processed:

  • Account and subscription information: Details supplied to create and manage your NDC account, credentials used for authentication, subscription status, payment transaction records and referral rewards.
  • Login and security information: Session tokens or identifiers, IP addresses, browser information and login activity used to verify access, manage sessions and protect accounts.
  • Extension settings: Preferences for uploading files and completing supported workflow steps.
  • Selected files: PDF and image documents chosen by the user for preparation and upload.
  • File metadata: Names, formats, sizes, and categories used to organize documents.
  • Document contents: Information contained in vehicle-registration documents selected by the user, processed as part of the document-preparation and VAHAN upload workflow. Processing these documents does not mean their contents are stored on the NDC server.

03 How We Use Information

Information is used as necessary to:

  • Authenticate users and enforce account permissions.
  • Manage subscriptions, payment transaction records and referral rewards.
  • Validate the document-upload process so the tool can follow the required upload steps.
  • Save extension preferences and settings.
  • Prepare, categorize, and optimize selected documents.
  • Support document uploading and related portal interactions.
  • Maintain service security and prevent unauthorized access.

We do not use document information for advertising or sell personal information to advertisers.

04 Storage & Security

Document privacy: NDC does not store your uploaded documents or the customer and bank details contained in those documents on our server. Your documents are submitted to the intended VAHAN portal only as part of your workflow. Extension settings may be stored in Chrome's extension storage, and documents may be processed locally for preparation and upload.

Workflow validation: Our server validates the document-upload process so the tool can follow the required upload steps. This validation does not mean your documents or their sensitive contents are stored on our server.

Account and access security: Authentication requests are sent to the NDC authentication service over HTTPS. Account, subscription, payment transaction and login security records are maintained separately to provide and protect your access. These records can include IP addresses and browser information; the document-privacy statement does not mean that NDC keeps no account or login records.

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the service. No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

05 Data Sharing

Authentication information is sent to the NDC authentication service when required to verify access and manage sessions. Documents are submitted to the intended VAHAN portal when the user carries out the document-upload workflow. NDC does not keep a server-side copy of those uploaded documents.

Subscription payments are processed through Razorpay. Payment details entered in its checkout are handled under Razorpay's privacy practices. NDC maintains payment transaction records to confirm purchases and manage subscriptions. Information is not sold to advertisers.

06 Data Retention

Locally stored settings may remain in the browser until cleared or the extension is removed. Uploaded documents and the customer or bank details contained in them are not retained on the NDC server. Server-side account information, subscription and payment transaction records, referral records, sessions and security logs may be retained as necessary for authentication, service administration, security, and applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods depend on the type of information and the operational requirements of the service.

07 Your Control

Users choose which files to select and submit. You can review your extension settings and remove locally stored extension data through Chrome's available extension-data controls.

For account-related or privacy requests, use the contact details provided below.
