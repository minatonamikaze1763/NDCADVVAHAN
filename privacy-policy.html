This policy applies to VAHAN NDC TOOL, a Chrome extension designed to help authorized users prepare, organize, and upload vehicle registration documents on supported VAHAN portal pages.

01 Overview

VAHAN NDC TOOL is developed as a productivity utility for document preparation and upload workflows. This policy explains the information the extension may process and how that information is used to provide its features.

02 Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, the extension may process:

  • Account information: Username and credentials supplied for authentication.
  • Session information: Tokens or identifiers used to maintain and verify an authorized session.
  • Extension settings: Preferences for uploading files and completing supported workflow steps.
  • Selected files: PDF and image documents chosen by the user for preparation and upload.
  • File metadata: Names, formats, sizes, and categories used to organize documents.
  • Document contents: Information contained in vehicle-registration documents selected by the user.

03 How We Use Information

Information is used as necessary to:

  • Authenticate users and enforce account permissions.
  • Save extension preferences and settings.
  • Prepare, categorize, and optimize selected documents.
  • Support document uploading and related portal interactions.
  • Maintain service security and prevent unauthorized access.

We do not use document information for advertising or sell personal information to advertisers.

04 Storage & Security

Extension settings may be stored in Chrome's extension storage. Selected documents may be processed locally by the extension before they are submitted to the destination selected by the user.

Authentication requests are sent to the NDC authentication service over HTTPS. The service may process account status, access permissions, session limits, and security information.

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the service. No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

05 Data Sharing

Authentication information is sent to the NDC authentication service when required to verify access and manage sessions. Documents are submitted to the relevant VAHAN portal when the user carries out the document-upload workflow.

Information is not sold to advertisers. Any additional sharing with third-party services will be subject to the actual features and data practices of the extension.

06 Data Retention

Locally stored settings may remain in the browser until cleared or the extension is removed. Server-side account information, sessions, and security logs may be retained as necessary for authentication, service administration, security, and applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods depend on the type of information and the operational requirements of the service.

07 Your Control

Users choose which files to select and submit. You can review your extension settings and remove locally stored extension data through Chrome's available extension-data controls.

For account-related or privacy requests, use the contact details provided below.

08 Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when the extension's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page along with a revised last-updated date.

09 Third-Party Service Disclaimer

VAHAN NDC TOOL is an independent third-party utility. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to the Government of India or the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways.
